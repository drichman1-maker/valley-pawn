The opportunity

Customers shop online before they ever walk in the door.

1

They search first

People look up "drill near me" or "used guitar" on their phone. If your stock isn't online, they find someone else's.

2

"Do you have…?" calls

Staff spend time on the phone describing what's in the case. A link answers that question before they dial.

3

Stock changes daily

Pawn and resale inventory turns over fast. A website is only useful if keeping it current takes minutes, not hours.

Under the hood

Built to be trusted with your business.

Secure by design

Only approved owner accounts can change anything. Hidden items are blocked by the database itself, not just the page.

All-or-nothing updates

An import either publishes completely or not at all. No half-updated catalog if something goes wrong.

Nothing gets lost

Items are archived, never deleted. Every publish saves a restore point.

Fast everywhere

Hosted on Vercel's global network with a Supabase database. Nothing to install or maintain in the shop.

Tested

More than 70 automated tests cover spreadsheet reading, publishing, restores, owner access and the storefront.

Accessible

Works with keyboards and screen readers, with readable type and strong contrast.
